# Teamtastic Self-Run Growth Engine

This plan copies the useful parts of the AI SEO and AI ad tools without paying for their wrappers.

## What the Tools Are Selling

BabyLoveGrowth focuses on automated SEO and AI search visibility: business analysis, a 30-day content plan, brand-aligned articles, automated publishing, authority backlinks, technical GEO audits, Reddit visibility, and LLM mention tracking.

Overrank.ai focuses on automated SEO content production: publishing several articles per week, Search Console tracking, AI visibility reporting, citation growth, and "autopilot" blog operations.

Zeely focuses on paid social creative: product URL to ad, UGC-style video scripts, static ad variants, hooks, CTAs, batch creative testing, AI audience suggestions, and campaign reporting.

The "sorro_seo" reference could not be verified from public search results under that exact name. Treat it as part of the same AI SEO/autopublishing category until we have a live URL.

## Teamtastic Version

1. Build topical authority around virtual team building, corporate game shows, remote engagement, Zoom games, virtual holiday parties, and HR event planning.
2. Publish 4-8 useful pages per month, each aimed at a buyer search or an AI answer prompt.
3. Add schema, FAQ blocks, clear internal links, and strong CTAs to the quiz and free game demo.
4. Track Search Console impressions, clicks, and pages with rising demand.
5. Create lightweight Instagram and LinkedIn ad batches from each article.
6. Repurpose the winners into short scripts, static ads, and sales emails.
7. Build ethical backlinks through partner pages, guest posts, podcast notes, directories, and event planning resource mentions.
8. Track whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude mention Teamtastic for core prompts.

## 30-Day Execution Plan

Week 1: technical and content foundation

- Ship `public/llms.txt` so AI crawlers and answer engines get a clean summary of Teamtastic.
- Add three high-intent blog pages and include them in the sitemap.
- Add FAQ schema to each new page.
- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Record current impressions, clicks, indexed pages, and top queries.

Week 2: content cluster

- Publish "Best Virtual Team Building Companies".
- Publish "Virtual Trivia for Work".
- Publish "Corporate Game Show Ideas".
- Publish "Team Building for Remote Engineering Teams".
- Add internal links from homepage, `/virtual-team-building`, `/resources`, and related blog posts.

Week 3: ads and social creative

- Make 10 static ad angles from the best-performing pages.
- Make 5 short video scripts using hooks from the articles.
- Run small-budget Meta tests only after conversion tracking is confirmed.
- Reuse winning copy in LinkedIn posts and email outreach.

Week 4: authority and measurement

- Pitch 20 HR/event planning blogs or directories for guest mentions.
- Answer 10 high-intent Reddit/Quora/LinkedIn discussions without spam.
- Run AI visibility checks for 10 prompts and log whether Teamtastic appears.
- Refresh the pages with the queries Search Console shows are rising.

## Rules

- Do not mass-publish thin AI articles.
- Do not buy spam backlinks.
- Do not pretend to have third-party awards or client stats.
- Every page should answer a real planner question and lead naturally to the quiz or demo.
- Refresh winners instead of only adding new pages.
