## 3. Best Virtual Team Building Companies
Source: `/blog/best-virtual-team-building-companies` (comparison/bottom-funnel intent — best for capturing people already shopping competitors)

**Instagram static ads**
1. "5 types of virtual team building companies (and which one you actually need)." — visual: simple icon grid (game show / self-service / escape room / trivia / workshop).
