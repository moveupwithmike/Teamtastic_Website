TEAMTASTIC CORPORATE HOLIDAY PARTY PLANNING CHECKLIST
====================================================

6–8 WEEKS BEFORE
[ ] Choose 2–3 possible dates and a time zone
[ ] Confirm approximate headcount and budget
[ ] Decide: holiday, winter, or inclusive year-end theme
[ ] Choose Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, or Webex
[ ] Reserve your live host and prime-time date

3–4 WEEKS BEFORE
[ ] Select the event format and 60-minute run of show
[ ] Gather company milestones, inside jokes, and trivia prompts
[ ] Choose teams, captains, prizes, or recognition moments
[ ] Confirm accessibility and global-participation needs
[ ] Send a calendar hold with a clear event description

1–2 WEEKS BEFORE
[ ] Approve custom questions, branding, and team names
[ ] Confirm final headcount and organizer contact
[ ] Test the video platform and screen-sharing permissions
[ ] Share joining instructions—players need no downloads
[ ] Confirm the host, event link, and backup contact

EVENT DAY
[ ] Open the room 15 minutes early
[ ] Keep the player link ready to paste into chat
[ ] Welcome the team and explain how scoring works
[ ] Capture a group screenshot only with permission
[ ] End with recognition, the final scoreboard, and a clear close

SAMPLE 60-MINUTE AGENDA
0:00–0:05  Welcome, tech check, and team setup
0:05–0:15  Fast holiday or year-in-review warm-up
0:15–0:30  Custom company trivia
0:30–0:42  Survey Showdown or music round
0:42–0:52  Team challenge and recognition moment
0:52–1:00  Final scores, awards, and group celebration
